I presented our paper "Legal Obligations for AI Explainability: Open Questions on Technical Methods" at The 4th World Conference on Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI-2026) in Fortaleza, Brazil. The link to the open-access paper is coming soon...

{% include figure.liquid path="/news/images/xai1.heic" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1 mb-3" %}

You can view the [presentation slides](/news/presentations/xai.pdf) here. This work was in collaboration with Prof. Karen Yeung and Paul Waller, originally funded by the European Lighthouse on Safe AI (ELSA). See the project details [here]({% link _projects/legalxai.md %}).

{% include figure.liquid path="/news/images/xai2.jpeg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1 mb-3" %}
